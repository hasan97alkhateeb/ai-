---
name: Subscription recovery rollout
description: Why live Whop checks do not replace authenticated customer verification
---
Keep the audited dashboard and authenticated-customer process until support has
validated provider corroboration against known cases in the intended environment.

**Why:** A Whop buyer identity does not prove ownership of a Clerk account.
Automated fixture verification cannot establish that customer relationship or
certify the provider's real-world termination behavior.

**How to apply:** Do not remove human ownership evidence or explicit canonical
approval based on passing automated tests. Provider failures must stop recovery,
not enable a manual bypass. No recovery automation may cancel or refund.
